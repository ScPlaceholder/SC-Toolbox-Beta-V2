ABERDEEN

Hurston, Stanton

Where some guests never want to leave. Others simply can't.

Aberdeen is a moon of bold contrasts: hot and hazy, orange and ochre, beautiful from orbit and considerably more demanding once you arrive.

Its thick, golden atmosphere wraps the surface like a blanket. Visitors are advised not to breathe the blanket.

Extended stays, fully arranged

Aberdeen is proudly home to the Klescher Rehabilitation Facility, one of Stanton's most talked-about long-term residences. Guests are met on arrival and provided with meals, uniforms and a structured daily schedule. Many describe the experience as life-changing. Most did not book it themselves.

For day visitors

If your plans are shorter, Aberdeen still rewards the curious. Watch the haze drift over rugged plains and admire a sky the colour of old brass. Enjoy the unique thrill of standing somewhere your suit is actively working to keep you alive.

Then return to your ship, free to go. On Aberdeen, that is a privilege worth savouring.

Travel note: Aberdeen's atmosphere is not breathable. For a guaranteed stay with air provided, see above.
