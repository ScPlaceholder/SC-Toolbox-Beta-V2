ARCCORP

Stanton

The great outdoors, finally indoors.

Some planets have forests. Some have oceans. ArcCorp looked at all that untidy variety and asked a bolder question: what if the whole world were a city?

Welcome to Stanton's shining ecumenopolis, where every horizon is a skyline and every meadow has been thoughtfully replaced with a parking structure.

Never lose your way again

On ArcCorp, there is no wilderness to get lost in. There is only the next block, and the block after that, gleaming all the way around. Visitors often say the view from orbit looks like a circuit board. We take that as a compliment.

Descend to Area18 and feel the pulse of a world that never clocks off. Neon glows through the night, which is also the day, depending on your district. Shop, dine and marvel at a planet that has turned "built-up area" into an art form.

A world managed with care

Every rooftop, every tower and every carefully permitted flight lane reflects the vision of ArcCorp itself. Here, nothing is left to chance, and very little is left to nature.

Travel note: Landing is permitted at designated zones only. The rest of the planet is extremely busy and would prefer not to be landed on.
