AREA18

ArcCorp, Stanton

Where every surface has something to say.

Step out of the spaceport into Area18 and let the city greet you, in glowing letters, from every direction at once.

This vibrant district of ArcCorp is a feast for the senses and a small workout for the eyes. Signs climb the towers, screens light the plazas, and every wall is eager to tell you about something you did not know you needed.

Shop until you are convinced

Area18 is a shopper's paradise. Browse weapons, armour, clothing and ship components, often within a few steps of each other. Merchants here are proud of their wares and happy to explain them, loudly, several times.

The city that never dims

Day or night, Area18 shines. Sit at a noodle stand, watch the crowds hurry beneath the towers, and let the endless glow remind you that sleep is a personal choice.

Look up, and the skyline keeps going. Look down, and the streets are busy too. Look anywhere at all, and there is probably an advertisement looking back.

Travel note: Visitors who find the lights overwhelming may close their eyes. The signs will wait.
