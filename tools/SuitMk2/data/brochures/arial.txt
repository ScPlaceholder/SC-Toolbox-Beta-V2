ARIAL

Hurston, Stanton

Your warmest welcome in Stanton.

Some destinations promise a warm welcome. Arial delivers one at the surface, around the clock, whether you asked for it or not.

This fiery moon of Hurston is the perfect escape for travelers who find other places a little chilly. Here, the ground glows with enthusiasm and the horizon shimmers with a heat that feels almost personal.

Soak it all in

Arial's sun-drenched landscape is ideal for the traveler who wants to feel something. Watch the air ripple above scorched rock. Listen to your suit's cooling system work harder than it ever has before. Feel your hull tick gently as it expands.

Pack light. Everything you bring will be warm by the time you land, including opinions you had not intended to share.

No crowds, ever

Unlike busier moons, Arial offers peace, privacy and a rare kind of solitude. The industrial outposts scattered across its surface keep to themselves, and most visitors keep their stays brief and memorable.

Travel note: Arial's surface is best appreciated from inside a climate-controlled vehicle. Some travelers report appreciating it most from orbit.
