BAIJINI POINT

ArcCorp, Stanton

The layover you'll tell your friends about. Briefly.

Every great journey has a pause in it. At Baijini Point, we have made the pause an art form.

This elegant orbital station hovers above ArcCorp, offering refuelling, repairs, shopping and some of the finest waiting in Stanton.

The art of passing through

Baijini Point is designed for travelers on the move. Grab a bite, pick up supplies, check your ship, and take a moment to appreciate the station's thoughtful corridors, which lead reliably to other corridors.

Watch a world glow

From the viewing windows, ArcCorp spreads out below like a jewel box left open. Admire it as long as you like. You will not need to land on it to see all of it, which many visitors consider the ideal arrangement.

Room to unwind

Settle into a quiet corner, watch ships arrive and depart, and savour that special station feeling of being almost somewhere. For many travelers it is the most relaxing part of the trip, precisely because nothing is expected of them.

Travel note: Hangar assignments are handled by station traffic control. Your patience is an important part of the process.
