CALLIOPE

microTech, Stanton

An epic destination. Literally.

Named for the muse of epic poetry, Calliope invites every visitor to become the hero of their own grand tale. Mostly, it is a tale about being cold.

Sing, traveler, of frozen plains

Here the snow lies deep and silent, and the wind, when it rises, recites long stanzas across the ice. Every step feels like a line of verse. Every breath fogs your visor like a dramatic pause.

Calliope's windswept landscape was made for journeys of great meaning. You will set out bravely. You will face hardship. You will return to your ship and turn up the heating, a transformed soul.

The epic, abridged

Not every visitor has time for a full saga. For the busy hero, we recommend the short edition: land, admire the view, lose the feeling in one toe, and depart with a story worth telling.

A moon with literary sisters

Calliope circles microTech alongside Clio and Euterpe. Each is named for a muse, and each seems quietly determined to live up to it.

Travel note: The heroic journey is optional. The thermal layers are not.
