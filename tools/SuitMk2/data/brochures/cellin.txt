CELLIN

Crusader, Stanton

Stanton's natural spa, now open to the public.

Some moons are cold and quiet. Cellin prefers to let off a little steam.

This small, rugged moon of Crusader is dotted with vents and geysers that release plumes of gas from deep below the surface. Where others see geology, we see wellness.

Unwind at the source

Imagine soaking in the warm, reassuring presence of an active landscape. Imagine the gentle hiss of the ground exhaling. Now imagine doing all of this from inside a sealed suit, which is strongly advised.

Cellin's vents have been releasing their energy for ages, and they will not stop just because you have arrived. That kind of consistency is hard to find in a spa.

Treatments on offer

The vents provide heat. The rocks provide exfoliation, particularly if you fall on them. The silence provides the rest.

A little something for the adventurous

When you have finished relaxing, visit Security Post Kareah in orbit, where some visitors come for the view and others come for disagreements.

Travel note: Please do not stand directly over a vent to feel its benefits. You will feel them very suddenly.
