CLIO

microTech, Stanton

Where history is made. Eventually.

Clio takes its name from the muse of history, and it has spent a very long time getting ready to have some.

Visitors arrive expecting great ruins and famous battlefields. They discover something more honest: a clean, frozen slate, with plenty of room for whatever happens next.

Be part of the record

Every footprint you leave on Clio's icy surface is, in a sense, a historical document. Every landing is a first chapter. Scholars of the future will study your visit closely, assuming nothing more exciting happens first.

A moon for thinkers

Clio's cool, uncluttered landscape encourages reflection. Research outposts dot the surface, where quiet teams study the natural world and take extremely detailed notes.

Please do not interrupt them. They are writing Clio's history, and you may be in it.

Tomorrow's heritage site, today

Why wait for a place to become famous? Come now, while the crowds are thin and the plaques have not been written. Future travelers will envy you, or at least never have heard of you.

Travel note: Clio's past is brief. Its present is cold. Its future depends largely on you, so please behave.
