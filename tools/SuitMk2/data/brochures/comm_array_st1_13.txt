COMM ARRAY ST1-13

Arial, Stanton

Love is in the air. So is everything else.

Somewhere between the blazing surface of Arial and the cool dark of space, a lone relay quietly carries the heartfelt words of Stanton. Every sweet message, every late-night apology and every "running late, sorry" passes through arrays like this one.

A romantic escape for two

Bring someone special to Comm Array ST1-13 and share a moment that is truly your own. There will be no crowds, no waiters and no one to overhear you, except perhaps in a technical sense.

Watch Arial glow like a banked fire below. Drift side by side in the silence. Let the array's blinking lights set the mood, because nothing else here is going to.

The perfect proposal spot

Couples searching for somewhere unforgettable need look no further. Pop the question beside a working piece of infrastructure, and your partner will never forget the moment, or its unusual setting.

Travel note: The array is not a hotel. Guests must remain in their own ships, which does limit the romance somewhat.
