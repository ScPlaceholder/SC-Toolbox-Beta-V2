COMM ARRAY ST1-48

Magda, Stanton

A landmark of functional design.

Architecture lovers, prepare to be moved. High above Magda floats Comm Array ST1-48, a structure that proves beauty can be found in pure purpose.

Every beam, every panel and every antenna exists for a reason. There are no flourishes, no decorative arches and no unnecessary spires. The array has one job, and it has dressed for it.

A study in form and function

Circle slowly and take in its bold industrial silhouette. Note the confident repetition of its components. Appreciate how the lights are placed exactly where lights are required, and nowhere else.

Critics have praised the array's honesty. It does not pretend to be a station, a monument or a shopping centre. It knows what it is, and after a visit, so will you.

A frame for the moon

From its vantage point, the array frames Magda beautifully, although it is fair to say the array was not thinking about that.

Travel note: Photography is encouraged. Guided tours are not available, as there is nobody to guide them.
