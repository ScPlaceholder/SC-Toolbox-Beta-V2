COMM ARRAY ST1-61

Hurston, Stanton

A learning adventure in orbit.

Looking for an educational outing the whole family can enjoy? Comm Array ST1-61 turns a simple visit into a lesson in how the verse stays connected.

Orbiting above Hurston, this unmanned relay helps carry messages across the region, keeping workers, families and businesses in touch with one another.

Fun facts for young explorers

Did you know that arrays like this one help pass along reports of crimes, so the proper authorities can respond? It's true. Every time the array is working, someone, somewhere, is being noticed. Isn't that exciting?

Hands-on learning, from a distance

Children can count the array's lights, sketch its antennas and guess which message might be passing through it at that very moment. Parents can use the visit to explain the importance of doing one job well, every day, without praise.

A lesson in discretion

The array will never tell you what it is carrying. That is part of its charm, and part of the lesson: some things are private, and some things are simply none of our business.

Travel note: Please keep all hands, tools and curious children inside the ship. It is not a climbing frame.
