COMM ARRAY ST1-92

Aberdeen, Stanton

Keeping hearts connected, however long the sentence.

Distance can be hard. So can time. Comm Array ST1-92 understands both.

Floating high above the golden haze of Aberdeen, this faithful relay helps keep messages moving across this corner of Stanton. For many families, arrays like this one are a lifeline, carrying news, good wishes and the occasional carefully worded update.

A symbol of hope

Aberdeen is home to some of Stanton's longest-staying residents. For their loved ones, a trip to ST1-92 is a way to feel closer. Look down at the moon and think: "Somewhere down there, someone is doing their best."

Reflect in the quiet

There are no visiting hours here, no guards and no forms to complete. Simply bring your ship alongside, gaze down at the amber clouds, and let the array's steady lights remind you that every sentence, in time, comes to an end.

A message worth sending

Before you leave, compose something kind. The array cannot promise when it will be read, but it will carry it faithfully, which is more than some people manage.

Travel note: The array cannot deliver care packages, cakes or files. Please stop asking.
