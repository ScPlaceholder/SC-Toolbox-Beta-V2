COMM ARRAY ST2-28

Cellin, Stanton

Your friendly neighbourhood watch, in orbit.

Every community needs someone keeping an eye out. For the space around Cellin, that someone is Comm Array ST2-28.

This humble relay helps pass along reports when trouble strikes, so that help can find its way to where it is needed. It does not carry a weapon, wear a badge or ask for thanks. It simply stays switched on, which, around here, is heroic enough.

A beloved local figure

The people of the area have strong feelings about ST2-28. Honest travelers find its presence deeply reassuring. Others visit it often, with tools, and appear to find it very interesting indeed.

A shining example of reliability

When the array is running, the neighbourhood is noticed. When it is not, the neighbourhood becomes, briefly, extremely relaxed. Maintenance crews work hard to keep that second kind of day a rare one.

Pay your respects

Fly by, tip your wings and thank ST2-28 for its service. It will not notice, but the gesture says a great deal about you.

Travel note: Visitors carrying hacking equipment are asked to reflect on their choices before approaching.
