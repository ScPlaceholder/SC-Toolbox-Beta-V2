COMM ARRAY ST2-47

Daymar, Stanton

Stanton's most exclusive night sky.

Tired of city glare? Weary of station floodlights? Comm Array ST2-47 invites you to rediscover the stars, interrupted only by a few small blinking lights of its own.

Above the dunes of Daymar, the array keeps a quiet orbit, far from the bustle of busy landing zones. Here the dark is deep, the stars are sharp, and the views reach all the way to the edge of the system.

An observatory without the building

Cut your engines, dim your cockpit and let your eyes adjust. Crusader fills a corner of the sky. Distant moons drift past like slow pearls. And every few seconds, the array blinks, reminding you gently that you are not the only thing out here.

Some stargazers find the blinking distracting. We prefer to think of it as punctuation.

A fellow watcher of the skies

The array faces the heavens every hour of every day, although it must be said it is paying attention to something else entirely.

Travel note: For the best viewing, position your ship so the array is behind you. It will not be offended.
