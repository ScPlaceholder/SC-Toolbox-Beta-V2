COMM ARRAY ST2-55

Crusader, Stanton

Say "I do" where the whole verse can hear you. Technically.

Your love story deserves a setting as grand as your feelings. Comm Array ST2-55 hangs above the rolling cloud tops of Crusader, offering a breathtaking backdrop for the most important day of your life.

A venue with a message

What could be more romantic than exchanging vows beside a structure devoted to communication? Every couple must learn to listen, to speak clearly and to pass things along without losing them. The array does all three, every day, and has never once asked for a honeymoon.

Your guests will marvel

Arrange your ships in a graceful circle around the array. Let the glow of the gas giant fill every cockpit. Share your vows over an open channel, so that everyone can hear, and several strangers can too.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

There is no catering, no dance floor and no seating plan to argue about. The array provides the atmosphere. You provide the love, the rings and, ideally, your own officiant.

Travel note: Bookings, reservations and decorations are not accepted. The array will be attending your wedding regardless.
