COMM ARRAY ST2-76

Yela, Stanton

Lovingly weathered. Honestly earned.

Some structures age gracefully. Comm Array ST2-76 ages memorably.

Positioned above the icy moon of Yela, within easy reach of the lively community at Grim HEX, this hardworking relay lives in a neighbourhood with character. If it bears a scorch mark or two, consider each one a story.

A heritage of resilience

Local tales say the array has been visited often by neighbours with strong opinions about who should hear what. It has been switched off, switched back on, argued over and restored. Through it all, it carries on with admirable composure.

Appreciate the patina

Fly in close and admire the array's rugged charm. Its steady lights may be the only well-behaved things for quite some distance. Its persistence is a lesson in carrying on.

Stay a while, carefully

Visitors are advised to keep their visits short, their weapons holstered and their eyes open. The array does not judge. Its neighbours sometimes do, quickly.

Travel note: If you find the array switched off, please do not assume it is closed for the season. Someone has simply had a busy afternoon.
