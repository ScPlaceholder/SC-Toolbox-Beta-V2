COMM ARRAY ST3-18

Lyria, Stanton

All-inclusive, with nothing to include.

At Comm Array ST3-18, every amenity is included in the price, and the price is nothing. Floating in peaceful orbit over Lyria, this all-inclusive retreat asks nothing of you and offers precisely that in return.

Your package includes

Unlimited views of Lyria's cold, pale surface. Round-the-clock access to the array's exterior. A private viewing of its status lights, available at every hour, for as long as your life support allows.

Also included: silence, darkness, the occasional passing ship, and the deep satisfaction of being near something important without having to do anything about it.

Not included

Food, drink, air, gravity, shelter, conversation, entertainment and a place to stand. Guests are warmly encouraged to bring these from home.

A resort that respects you

There are no upsells here, no hidden fees and no one knocking on your hatch to offer a massage. The array trusts you to create your own experience, and then it goes back to work.

Travel note: ST3-18 does not offer check-out. Simply leave. The array will not notice, and will not charge you for it.
