COMM ARRAY ST3-35

Wala, Stanton

Open your heart. Adopt an array.

Out above the sleepy moon of Wala, a lonely relay works tirelessly, day after day, without a single visitor, compliment or name of its own. Only a number: ST3-35.

It doesn't have to be this way.

A small gesture, a big difference

When you visit Comm Array ST3-35, you are not just sightseeing. You are showing a humble piece of infrastructure that someone, somewhere, noticed. The array may not be able to feel appreciated. That is exactly why it deserves to be.

What adoption looks like

Fly past it on your way somewhere else. Wave from the cockpit. Tell your friends about it. Some supporters even give their array a private nickname, though we ask that you do not paint it on.

Your support matters

Every day, ST3-35 carries messages it will never read, from people it will never meet, to places it will never go. It asks nothing in return. Surely that deserves a visit.

Travel note: Adoption is purely emotional. The array remains the property of its operators and cannot come home with you.
