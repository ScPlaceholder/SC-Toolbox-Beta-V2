COMM ARRAY ST4-31

Calliope, Stanton

Stanton's most atmospheric haunt.

Late at night, above the frozen face of Calliope, anyone listening to an open channel may hear things. Crackles. Murmurs. Half a word, then nothing. Some call it interference. We call it an experience.

A ghost tour in orbit

Pull alongside Comm Array ST4-31, dim your cabin lights, and tune your receiver to the static. Listen closely. Somewhere in the hiss, you may hear a voice. It is almost certainly just someone ordering parts, but in the dark, who can say?

The chill of the unknown

The icy glow of Calliope lights the array from below, casting long shadows across its frame. The silence is total. The cold is absolute. Even the bravest travelers feel a small shiver, although this may be their heating.

For thrill-seekers of all ages

Bring friends, bring blankets and bring your best spooky stories. The array will provide the eerie blinking, free of charge, all night long.

Travel note: No ghosts have ever been confirmed at ST4-31. None have ever been ruled out, either.
