COMM ARRAY ST4-64

Euterpe, Stanton

See one in the wild.

Nature lovers, this is your moment. High above the quiet moon of Euterpe, a comm array can be observed in its natural habitat. It goes about its routine exactly as it would if no one were watching.

A shy and patient creature

Comm Array ST4-64 is a solitary species. It does not form herds, build nests or migrate. It prefers to remain in one orbit for its entire life, calling out steadily to others of its kind across the dark.

Approach slowly and you may witness its famous display: a rhythmic blinking of lights, performed for no audience at all.

Wildlife watching etiquette

Please do not feed the array. Please do not attempt to tag, collar or relocate it. Keep a respectful distance, switch off unnecessary lights, and let it carry on undisturbed.

A thrilling sighting

Many travelers go their whole lives without truly noticing a comm array. Spotting ST4-64 up close is a quiet privilege, and one you will want to tell someone about. Conveniently, it can pass the message along.

Travel note: ST4-64 is not endangered. It is, however, rarely appreciated, which may be worse.
