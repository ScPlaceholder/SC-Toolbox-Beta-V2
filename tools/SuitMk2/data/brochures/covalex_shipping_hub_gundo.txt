COVALEX SHIPPING HUB GUNDO

Daymar, Stanton

Go behind the scenes of your delivery.

Have you ever wondered where your package goes when it is "in transit"? Wonder no more. It may very well be here, orbiting Daymar, having a quiet moment of its own.

Welcome to the Covalex Shipping Hub Gundo, where boxes arrive, boxes wait and boxes, in time, depart.

The romance of logistics

Stroll among the neatly stacked cargo and feel the steady heartbeat of Stanton commerce. Every crate here has a destination, a purpose and a label, which is more than many travelers can say.

Couriers and haulers are the true stars at Gundo. Watch them collect parcels, confirm manifests and set off with the calm focus of people who have signed for a great deal in their lives.

A view with a delivery window

From the hub, the sandy face of Daymar turns below, a warm reminder that every package, eventually, has to go somewhere.

Travel note: Visitors are asked not to open, shake or sit on any parcels. If you find a box with your name on it, it is probably not a gift.
