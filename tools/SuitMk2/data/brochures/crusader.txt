CRUSADER

Stanton

Come for the clouds. Stay above them.

Most destinations make a great first impression with the ground. Crusader has skipped that step entirely.

This magnificent gas giant, home of Crusader Industries, offers endless skies, soft pastel horizons and absolutely nothing to stand on. Its visitors don't mind. They are too busy floating.

The sky is not the limit

On Crusader, the sky is the whole destination. Drift past towering cloudbanks, admire the gentle light, and let the planet's peaceful atmosphere remind you that solid footing is overrated.

Fortunately, the city of Orison offers elegant platforms suspended high among the clouds, so you will always have somewhere to put your luggage. Crusader Industries builds some of the most graceful ships in the verse here. A company surrounded by sky learns to take flight seriously.

A family of moons

Cellin, Daymar and Yela circle close by, each ready to offer the ground that Crusader tastefully declined to provide.

Travel note: Do not attempt to descend below the cloud platforms in search of a surface. Many have tried. The clouds have been very polite about it.
