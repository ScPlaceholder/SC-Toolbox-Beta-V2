DAYMAR

Crusader, Stanton

All the beach. None of the water.

Picture golden dunes stretching to the horizon, a wide sky above, and warm sand as far as the eye can see. Now stop picturing the ocean. There isn't one.

Welcome to Daymar, the sandy moon of Crusader and Stanton's most honest seaside getaway. It has kept everything you love about the beach and gracefully let go of everything wet.

Sand, reimagined

Daymar's dunes and rocky flats are perfect for ground vehicles, and adventurous travelers love racing across them. The sand here gets into everything. Regulars describe this as part of the charm, and first-timers describe it in other ways.

Take a long drive. Watch Crusader loom huge in the sky. Find a quiet ridge and enjoy a sunset that no swimsuit could improve.

Explore the outposts

Daymar is home to scattered outposts and mining sites, each offering a friendly welcome, a landing pad and, frequently, more sand.

Travel note: Sunscreen is recommended. So is shaking out your boots. Several times.
