EUTERPE

microTech, Stanton

The music of the spheres, now with outdoor seating.

Sharing its name with the muse of music, Euterpe is the quiet artist of microTech's family. It does not shout. It does not sparkle. It simply waits, like a great concert hall before the first note.

Listen closely

Stand still on Euterpe and hear its signature performance: the soft crunch of your own boots, the hum of your suit, and the gentle rhythm of your breathing. Critics have called it minimal. We call it intimate.

Travelers who bring their own music find the acoustics exceptional. Nothing out here competes with your favourite songs, interrupts your chorus or asks you to turn it down.

An encore every night

When darkness falls, microTech hangs in the sky above, and the whole moon feels like a stage after the audience has gone home. For some, it is lonely. For true music lovers, it is the best seat in the house.

A performance for one

There is no ticket office, no dress circle and no one coughing in the quiet passages. Every visitor gets a private recital, whether they wanted one or not.

Travel note: Humming is permitted on Euterpe. Applause is appreciated but will not be heard.
