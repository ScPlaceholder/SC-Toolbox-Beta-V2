EVERUS HARBOR

Hurston, Stanton

A safe harbour in a sea of stars.

Every sailor needs a harbour. Every pilot needs a hangar. Everus Harbor proudly offers both, although only one of them is technically true.

Set sail for comfort

Perched in orbit above Hurston, Everus Harbor welcomes weary captains with open docking bays, friendly services and the reassuring smell of fuel and freshly repaired hulls.

Drop anchor, figuratively. Stretch your legs, restock your hold, and enjoy the sensation of being somewhere that is not the inside of your cockpit.

The finest view of Hurston

From the station's windows, Hurston turns slowly below, its hazy surface glowing in shades of brown and gold. It is a magnificent sight. One of the great advantages of Everus Harbor is that you can enjoy it without inhaling any of it.

Any port in a storm

Heading down to Lorville, out to the moons or onward across Stanton? Everus Harbor is the perfect place to catch your breath before the next leg of your voyage.

Travel note: No boats are available for hire at Everus Harbor. We are asked about this more often than you would think.
