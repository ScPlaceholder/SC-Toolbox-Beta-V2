GRIM HEX

Yela, Stanton

Come as you are. Leave with whatever you can carry.

Tucked into an asteroid in the belt around Yela, Grim HEX is Stanton's most discreet destination. There are no dress codes here, no awkward questions and, frankly, no reliable way to know who anyone is.

Hospitality, with fewer rules

Grim HEX welcomes travelers from every walk of life, especially the walks that avoid main roads. Whether you are between jobs, between identities or between legal situations, you will find a friendly face here, or at least a face.

Pre-owned treasures

Browse a colourful selection of goods with rich and varied histories. Many items arrive at Grim HEX after long and unexpected journeys, and their previous owners would be astonished to see them.

Why choose Grim HEX? The comfort of anonymity. The thrill of the unknown. The quiet satisfaction of a station where the security is exactly as strong as you are.

A community of independents

The residents of Grim HEX value freedom, self-reliance and personal space, particularly the space around their ships.

Travel note: Keep your hand near your belongings at all times. Ideally, keep your belongings in your hand.
