HURSTON

Stanton

A planet that means business.

Some worlds were shaped by wind and water. Hurston was shaped by Hurston Dynamics, and frankly, it shows.

Welcome to the proud industrial heart of Stanton, where the mines run deep and the factories run late. The air has a character you will remember long after you have left.

Authentic in every breath

Forget filtered, sanitised resort worlds. On Hurston, the atmosphere is rich, textured and full of personality. Locals say you stop noticing it after a while. Visitors say they notice it for weeks.

Take in the vast stripped plains, a landscape that reflects generations of hard work. Visit Lorville, the proud company city where every street, every tram and every paycheck belongs to one big family business.

Built to last

Hurston Dynamics has supplied weapons and heavy industry across the verse for generations. When you tour their home world, you see that commitment up close, sometimes closer than you had planned.

Four moons, Aberdeen, Arial, Ita and Magda, keep the planet company and share in its hardworking spirit.

Travel note: Visitors are welcome to breathe freely. Some prefer to breathe carefully.
