ITA

Hurston, Stanton

Small in name. Big in being there.

With a name only three letters long, Ita does not waste your time, and neither will this brochure.

Ita is one of Hurston's four moons, and it takes that responsibility seriously. It rises. It sets. It is always, reliably, exactly where the charts say it will be.

The joy of the understated

Not every destination needs a skyline or a famous landmark. Ita offers something rarer: rock, and plenty of it, arranged with quiet confidence in every direction.

Stroll the surface and let the simplicity wash over you. Pick up a stone. Look at it. Put it down again. On Ita, these small moments become the whole itinerary.

Some visitors ask whether that is the entire tour. We prefer to call it focused.

For the jaded explorer

After the haze of Hurston and the heat of Arial, Ita is a gentle palate cleanser. Nothing here will surprise you, and that, we promise, is the point.

Travel note: Ita's attractions may be viewed in any order. There are not many, so you will not get lost.
