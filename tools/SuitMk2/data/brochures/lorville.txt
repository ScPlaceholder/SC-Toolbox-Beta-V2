LORVILLE

Hurston, Stanton

You're not just visiting. You're onboarding.

Welcome to Lorville, the proud capital of Hurston and the beating heart of the Hurston Dynamics family. Here, everyone has a place, and most of those places come with a job title.

A city with a purpose

Lorville was built for work, and it has never pretended otherwise. Towering structures rise over wide industrial districts, trains carry commuters to and from their shifts, and the skyline hums with dedication.

Visitors will notice the city's walls and gates. They are a testament to good planning. Everyone is where they are supposed to be, and the company likes it that way.

A skyline of character

Lorville's golden haze gives every sunset a warm, industrial glow. Some visitors call it smog. Locals simply call it the weather.

Loyalty is its own reward

Wander the business district, take in the views, and feel the pride of a city where one company has thought of everything. Your comfort matters to Hurston Dynamics. So does your productivity.

Travel note: Security personnel are here to help. Please make sure you are someone they want to help.
