LYRIA

ArcCorp, Stanton

Get away from it all. Then dig.

After a few days in the endless city of ArcCorp, travelers often find themselves longing for open space and ground that has not been paved. Lyria was waiting.

This cool, pale moon offers everything the big city cannot: silence, distance and rocks that nobody has built a tower on yet.

The working holiday

Lyria is a favourite with miners, and it is easy to see why. The ground here is generous, the sky is wide, and the minerals are simply lying underneath everything, waiting to be appreciated.

Why merely visit a landscape when you could extract it? Bring a mining vehicle and a sense of purpose. Leave with a full hold and a deeper connection to the ground, literally.

Cold comfort

Lyria runs brisk, so wrap up well. The chill is part of the character, and the character is mostly chill.

A view of home

From the surface, ArcCorp glows on the horizon, a single luminous world of light. It is never more beautiful than when you are standing somewhere else.

Travel note: Lyria's rocks are for everyone. Mining claims and local regulations may disagree.
