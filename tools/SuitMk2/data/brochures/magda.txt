MAGDA

Hurston, Stanton

Picture-perfect from every angle. Especially the same one.

Photographers, this is your moon. Magda offers wide open vistas, soft light and a landscape so consistent you can take a hundred pictures without ever worrying which one is best.

They are all equally Magda.

Frame the quiet

Magda's broad, uncluttered horizons make every shot a study in minimalism. There are no crowds, no tangled cityscapes and no inconvenient landmarks competing for attention. Just you, the ground and the sky, meeting exactly where they always do.

Serious artists set up at dawn and stay until dusk, waiting for the perfect moment. On Magda, the perfect moment is extremely patient. It will be there tomorrow, looking the same.

A companion for Hurston

Magda offers a welcome break from the factories of its parent world. Look up from the surface and you may spot Hurston itself, the only thing on the horizon that changes the subject.

Travel note: Visitors are encouraged to label their images before returning home. Nobody will be able to tell them apart otherwise.
