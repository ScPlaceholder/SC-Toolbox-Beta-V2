MICROTECH

Stanton

Innovation, served chilled.

There are cold planets, and then there is microTech, a world that took the idea of a crisp morning and extended it indefinitely.

Welcome to the home of one of the verse's great technology companies, a planet of snowfields, glittering forests and air so fresh it arrives with a warning.

A winter that commits

Other destinations offer a winter season. microTech offers a winter lifestyle. Pack warmly, then pack again, and discover a landscape that has made its peace with being frozen.

The planet's climate is a little brisker than some early projections suggested. Locals prefer to call it character.

Descend to New Babbage, the elegant corporate city where sleek towers and gleaming domes keep everything comfortably warm, glossy and on message. Step outside only if you are confident in your outerwear and your life choices.

Three moons of inspiration

Calliope, Clio and Euterpe, named for the muses of legend, circle overhead. microTech saw no reason its moons should be any less cultured than its software.

Travel note: Exposed skin is not recommended outdoors. Neither is standing still, lingering, or being optimistic about the forecast.
