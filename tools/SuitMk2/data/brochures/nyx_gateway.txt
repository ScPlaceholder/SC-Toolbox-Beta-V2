NYX GATEWAY

Stanton

The door to somewhere else entirely.

Some journeys take you somewhere new. Nyx Gateway takes you somewhere different, and there is an important difference.

Stationed near the jump point to Nyx, this gateway is the last well-lit stop before the frontier. Here, travelers pause, top up, and take one final look at the tidy, regulated comfort they are about to leave behind.

For the free-spirited traveler

Beyond the gateway lies Nyx, a system with a reputation for independence, hardy settlers and very few people telling you what to do. Whether you find that liberating or alarming depends on who you are, and possibly on who is behind you.

Everything for the road

Before you jump, the gateway offers a warm meal, a full tank and a chance to reconsider your cargo manifest. Seasoned travelers recommend stocking up generously. Out there, the corner shop is rather far away.

Pause before the leap

Take a moment at the viewing windows. Stanton glows warmly behind you. Ahead, the dark waits, patient and full of possibilities.

Travel note: Gateway staff cannot advise on what lies beyond the jump. They have, for the most part, stayed on this side.
