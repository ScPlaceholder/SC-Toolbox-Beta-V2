ORISON

Crusader, Stanton

Walk on air. Well, near it.

High in the clouds of Crusader, a city floats. Its streets are platforms, its parks drift through the sky, and its views are simply unbeatable, because there is nothing underneath to block them.

Welcome to Orison, the most elegant way in Stanton to live above your means.

A sky-high garden

Orison's floating platforms are graced with lush greenery and soft light. Stroll among the treetops, admire a horizon of cream and rose, and enjoy the breeze of a city that is quite literally up in the air.

Where ships are born

Orison is home to Crusader Industries, and its great shipyards share the sky. Look out from the right platform and you may see a hull taking shape among the clouds.

A word about looking down

Some visitors like to peer over the railings into the deep clouds below. We encourage them to do this only once, and then to go and sit somewhere with a cushion.

Travel note: Items dropped in Orison are considered donations to the atmosphere.
