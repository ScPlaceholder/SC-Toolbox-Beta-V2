PORT TRESSLER

microTech, Stanton

Your last warm room before the snow.

Above the frozen world of microTech, Port Tressler keeps the lights on, the heaters humming and the welcome mat defrosted.

Think of it as the mountain lodge of Stanton. Here, travelers gather to refuel, restock and bravely discuss whether they really need to go down there.

The lodge above the slopes

Port Tressler offers everything a pilot needs before descending to microTech: hangars, shops, supplies and a final chance to reconsider your choice of jacket. Browse the stores, top up your ship, and enjoy a warm meal while you can still feel your fingers.

Look out on wonder

The station's windows frame microTech in all its frosty glory, a white world gleaming against the dark. From up here, it looks peaceful, pristine and inviting. From down there, it looks exactly the same, only much colder.

A place to come back to

Many visitors describe the trip back up to Port Tressler as the best part of their microTech experience. We take that as high praise, and only slightly as a warning.

Travel note: Please remove snow from your boots before entering the concourse. It has come a very long way and would like to go home.
