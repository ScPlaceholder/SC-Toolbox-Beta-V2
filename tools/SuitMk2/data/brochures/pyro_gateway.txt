PYRO GATEWAY

Stanton

Adventure starts here. So do the disclaimers.

For the pilot who has done Stanton twice and wants something bolder, Pyro Gateway stands at the edge of the known, and slightly beyond the insured.

Just past this station lies the jump point to Pyro, a system famous for its temperamental star, its abandoned industry and its spirited local population. It is not for everyone. That is the whole point.

The last word in preparation

Pyro Gateway is your final chance to refuel, repair, restock and write any messages you may wish to have sent later. Our services are designed to give you every advantage before you go. We strongly recommend taking all of them.

Know before you go

Pyro's star is known for its dramatic flares. Its outposts are known for their independent attitudes. Its visitors are known, mostly, for coming back with stories, and occasionally for coming back.

A gateway, not a guarantee

Whatever awaits on the other side, one thing is certain. The windows here offer a lovely view of the jump point, and of everyone who returns through it.

Travel note: Pyro Gateway assumes no responsibility for anything that happens after you leave the docking ring. It assumes limited responsibility before that, too.
