SECURITY POST KAREAH

Cellin, Stanton

Where every visitor has a story. Some have files.

Orbiting quietly above Cellin, Security Post Kareah stands as a symbol of law, order and administrative thoroughness. Its corridors are clean, its lights are bright, and its records are extensive.

A monument to peace, most days

Kareah was built to keep the neighbourhood around Crusader safe. On calm days, it does exactly that. On livelier days, visitors may witness spirited exchanges between those who uphold the law and those who have come to discuss it urgently.

A place of second chances

For some travelers, Kareah offers a rare opportunity to revisit the past. Its record systems take a central place in the life of the region, and a surprising number of visitors take a keen personal interest in them. We admire their commitment to accurate paperwork.

Plan your visit

Arrive with a clear purpose, a steady nerve and preferably some friends. Kareah rewards the prepared, and it remembers the unprepared for a very long time.

Travel note: Weapons discharged inside the station may affect your visit, your record and your ability to plan future visits.
