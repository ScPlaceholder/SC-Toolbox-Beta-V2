SERAPHIM STATION

Crusader, Stanton

A heavenly stop above the clouds.

With a name borrowed from the angels and a view of Crusader's endless clouds, Seraphim Station feels a little closer to paradise. The fuel prices, regrettably, remain earthly.

Rise above it all

Floating serenely in orbit, Seraphim Station offers pilots a moment of grace between destinations. Dock, rest and let the rosy glow of Crusader below wash over you like a blessing.

A choir of services

Here, the angels have practical skills. Repairs, refuelling, shopping and dining are all close at hand. Some visitors say the hangar doors open with a sound that is almost holy.

Gateway to the cloud city

Seraphim is the natural first stop for travelers bound for Orison, the floating city below. Many make the descent with a sense of wonder. Some make it with a sense of vertigo. Both are welcome.

A little peace before the next leg

Sit by the windows, watch the storms swirl far beneath you, and enjoy the rare feeling of looking down on weather.

Travel note: Seraphim Station cannot answer prayers or waive fees. Please ask at the service desk anyway.
