SHEPHERD’S REST

Bloom, Pyro III

The harvest at the heart of Pyro!

You’ve seen Pyro’s abandoned mines. You’ve heard about its solar flares. Now come discover its best-kept secret: people are growing things here. On purpose.

Welcome to Shepherd’s Rest, the farming outpost that looked at Bloom’s cold ground, unpredictable skies, and long history of mining and said, “Perfect. Let’s plant something.”

A breath of fresh air. An absolutely extraordinary crop.

Step off your ship into Bloom’s breathable atmosphere and take in a sight you won’t soon forget: a working farm where every harvest is a small triumph over the planet’s name.

Watch the outpost at work. Browse the local shop. Bring cargo space for the commodity terminal, or simply come to marvel at the determination required to coax a living from Pyro III. At Shepherd’s Rest, even an ordinary day in the fields feels like an achievement.

An unforgettable frontier escape

Elsewhere in Pyro, travelers visit the remains of what people left behind. At Shepherd’s Rest, you can see what people are building next. There’s no finer place to pause your journey, look out over a hard-won farm, and say, “I cannot believe they grow food here.”

Set course for Shepherd’s Rest. Bloom may have gotten its name a little early. These folks are working to make it true.

Travel note: Shepherd’s Rest is a working outpost. Check Pyro conditions before departure and make your own onward travel arrangements.
