TERRA GATEWAY

Stanton

Refinement is just one jump away.

There are gateways, and then there is Terra Gateway. Poised at the threshold of Terra, it serves the discerning traveler bound for one of the most prosperous systems in the verse. Accordingly, it asks visitors to look their best.

Prepare to be impressed

Before you make the jump, take a moment to freshen up. Straighten your flight suit. Polish your helmet. Consider, just for a moment, whether your ship is really the one you want to be seen arriving in.

The finishing school of Stanton

Terra Gateway offers the full range of services a pilot needs, delivered with a quiet polish. Refuel, restock and enjoy a moment of calm among fellow travelers who all seem to be heading somewhere important.

Leave the dust behind

Whether you are coming from the factories of Hurston or the dunes of Daymar, this is the ideal spot to leave a little of Stanton behind. Preferably not on the upholstery.

A word on manners

Terra is a place of culture and distinction. Practise your good posture here, where the stakes are lower.

Travel note: Terra Gateway does not enforce a dress code. It simply notices.
