WALA

ArcCorp, Stanton

For the traveler who does not want to talk about it.

ArcCorp is loud, bright and busy. Wala is its quieter sibling, the one who sits in the corner at family gatherings and seems perfectly content.

Here, no one will ask about your travel plans. No one will ask anything, really. Wala's sparsely settled landscape is the perfect retreat for anyone who has had enough of other people.

The art of keeping to yourself

Wala's surface stretches out in muted tones, broken only by the occasional industrial site going about its business. Even the outposts here seem introverted. They have landing pads and doors, but they are not going to make a fuss about it.

Spend the day wandering. Spend the evening watching ArcCorp shimmer overhead, grateful for every kilometre between you and it.

Recharge your social battery

Visitors return from Wala rested, refocused and ready to face crowds again. Or at least ready to face one or two people, briefly, with a nod.

Travel note: Wala offers limited services. Travelers seeking conversation should bring their own, or a ship's computer that talks too much.
