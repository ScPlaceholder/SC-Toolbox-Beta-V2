YELA

Crusader, Stanton

Put a ring on it.

Every moon wants to stand out. Yela did something about it. This icy jewel of Crusader wears a glittering belt of asteroids, and wears it with confidence.

Ice, and then more ice

Yela's frozen surface is a winter paradise of white plains, pale ridges and sparkling frost. It is crisp, bright and unforgettable, especially for travelers who arrive dressed for Daymar.

Skate, slide or simply stand very still and admire the view. Yela rewards all three, although standing still is the one most visitors manage.

The ring, up close

Yela's asteroid belt is a sight to behold from orbit, and an even closer sight if you fly through it carelessly. Admire the tumbling rocks. Respect the tumbling rocks. Please keep a comfortable distance from the tumbling rocks.

Colourful neighbours

Tucked into the belt, the outpost of Grim HEX welcomes independent-minded travelers of every kind. Its residents are known for their generous spirit, flexible attitudes toward ownership and a lively approach to resolving disputes.

Travel note: Visitors to the ring are advised to lock their ships. Visitors to Grim HEX are advised to lock them twice.
